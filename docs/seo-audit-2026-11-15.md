## What is good

## What to fix (in order of value)
1. **133 titles and 70 descriptions are too long** (mostly product pages: 90+ character titles). Google cuts them off, so the keyword and the click reason are lost.
2. **53 older service-in-city pages (Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune) have no text of their own** (about 440 words of shared copy, FAQ markup on only 12 of 65). The Auto Pages upgrade step writes proper versions.
3. **Product pages (/events/...) mention Hyderabad in titles** even though the page serves every city.
4. **Thin pages:** the gallery and the cities hub have little text.
5. **Product pages use "Service" structured data**; "Product" structured data with price is the richer option.
